Store & fishing (next)
- Cooler gear + fish spoilage. Bucket -> Cooler -> Ice chest; fish lose value after a few days without one. Freshness hints already ship in sale pop-ups and the wallet, so turn spoilage on only after they've been live a while.
- More consumables, each loaded onto your next cast like worms: tide chart (privately tells you today's/tomorrow's tide), fish finder (how fished-out the lake is), shiny spinner (2x rare odds), deep-water jig (heavier fish), megalodon bait (5x megalodon odds, pricey), night permit (one extra cast today), chum bucket (everyone's next cast today gets a bonus, and the chat finds out who chummed).
- Poaching and a wanted level. Fish over a limit, out of season, or protected species for extra beans; your wanted level rises, and the game warden can fine you, confiscate your bucket, or ban you from the lake for a few days.
- Black market. Buys what the store won't (poached fish, protected species) and sells shady gear, at bad rates. Ties into the wanted level.
- Two-person reels. Some big fish can't be landed alone: the cast posts "something huge is on the line" and another person has a short window to tap Help, or it gets away. Both get credit or a split.
- Seasonal and time-based fish. Species that only bite in certain months, at night, or on certain tides/weekdays; a hint system so people learn the patterns.
- Awards / museum. Donate your first of each species to a chat museum; collection progress, trophies for records, "first megalodon" honors.
- Gator mechanics. A gator in the lake steals fish from buckets and might eat the pet; gator-proof gear, or a bounty to hunt it.
- Headlines. Absurd newspaper headlines for big moments: "LOCAL MAN LANDS MEGALODON, CLAIMS IT WAS 'MOSTLY TECHNIQUE'".
